Developer

API keys for live data. They stay on this phone and are never in the app’s public code.

🚗 Live traffic

Without a key, drive times use free road data plus Melbourne rush-hour estimates. A free key from developer.tomtom.com adds live traffic.

🚆 Train disruptions

Free key from opendata.transport.vic.gov.au (sign in, then API keys). Shows Metro train suspensions and replacement buses on the map; the key stays on this phone.

Google Sheet

Live flights shared by all your devices, and your shift log month by month

📗 Connection

Easiest: open the phone link from the sheet (✈️ Offer Pilot ▸ 3. Phone link) once on each device and both boxes fill in. Flights: the sheet calls AeroDataBox every hour and every pilot reads them from the sheet. Shift log: each shift goes to a tab for its month (“2026-10 October”), with a Summary tab of the months. Both stay only on this phone and in your own Google Drive.

⏱ Shift log

Each row: date, punch in and out, hours, car, trips, offers (accepted and declined, ride types), fares, costs, take-home, $/hr, target, km, where you started and finished, areas, a route map link and the offers list. Sending again updates the same row.

📍 Route log

A point every ~100 m while the pilot is open. Older routes are removed from the phone; shift totals and the sheet's route links stay.
